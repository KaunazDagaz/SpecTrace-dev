The rules count only statements that carry an uppercase BCP 14 keyword, so recall is not split into gold requirements with and without a keyword.

Matching: at least 50% overlap of the shorter span; each gold requirement matches at most one claim and each claim at most one gold requirement; pairs are taken greedily by overlap size, largest first; ties go to the gold requirement earlier in the document, then to the earlier claim; a claim whose quote is found more than once may match through any occurrence. Counting: a claim counts only through a quote located in the source; a claim whose quote cannot be located, or that has no quote, is a false positive.
